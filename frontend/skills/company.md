# Company Profile

## Website Name
TalentLens Atlas

## Brand Positioning
TalentLens Atlas helps hiring teams compare resumes against job descriptions and understand candidate-job fit with clear, actionable analysis.

## Product Vision
- Turn resume screening into a transparent and explainable process.
- Show strengths, gaps, and role alignment in one place.
- Make hiring collaboration faster with readable insights.

## Core Experience Principles
- Clear first: every score should be understandable.
- Human review first: AI supports decisions, it does not replace them.
- Visual clarity: dashboards should guide the eye to what matters.
- Fast workflows: reduce time from upload to shortlist.

## Tone and Voice
- Confident and practical
- Insightful but not noisy
- Professional with a modern editorial feel

## Phase 1 Frontend Direction
- Build a multi-route mock product experience.
- Use mock data for resumes, jobs, and analysis summaries.
- Focus on elegant design, responsive layout, and smooth interactions.
- Keep backend integration out of scope for this phase.
